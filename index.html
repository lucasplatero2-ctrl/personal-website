Engineering & Inventions

I have an inventing hobby, as well as a desire to manifest the ideas in my mind into reality. There are plenty of examples, but my favorite is my still work in progress design of a spinning armillary of the Solar System.

I call this one Solar Semblance, and it works as an 8 sided box that opens up to showcase each of the planets revolving around the sun. It is still in its initial phases of manifestation, but solving the engineering problems to bring it into fruition is one of the most satisfying things I've ever done!

Writing & Stories

Writing is another way I like to give an actualization of my imagination. My ideal work is something one can go back to to reread and never get bored of doing so. I have many stories I like to work on, but here I showcase my three proudest aspects of writing:

The first is The Tiger of Rakshaw, which is a fully fledged science fiction novel. I haven't got to publishing it since I keep going back to edit it to liken it more to what I have imagined.

The second is Ominous Positivity, a short story that tackles heavy themes and turns into a sort of cosmic horror that I enjoy rereading.

The third is a link to my instagram page, where I post politically charged poetry and fun pictures of myself.
